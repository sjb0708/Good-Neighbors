Child Safety Standards

Costa Blanca Connect · Costa Blanca Villas, Farallón, Coclé, Panamá · Last updated: June 4, 2026

1. Our Commitment

Costa Blanca Connect is a private community platform restricted to verified adult residents, renters, and approved local businesses of Costa Blanca Villas. We have a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) material, grooming behavior, and any content that endangers minors. This policy applies to every account, every post, every comment, every message, and every uploaded image or video on our platform.

2. Membership and Age Restrictions

Accounts on Costa Blanca Connect are restricted to individuals 18 years of age or older. Users affirm their age during signup as part of the Member Agreement. Accounts found to belong to minors are immediately suspended and reported to community administrators.

3. Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate, permanent account termination and reporting to the appropriate authorities:

  • Any depiction of child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Sexualized images, descriptions, or discussions involving minors
  • Grooming behavior or attempts to contact minors for sexual purposes
  • Sharing or soliciting personal contact information of minors
  • Encouragement, glorification, or normalization of harm to children
  • Solicitation, trafficking, or exploitation of any kind

4. Reporting Mechanism

Every post, comment, message, and user profile on Costa Blanca Connect includes an in-app Report action accessible from the action menu (three-dot icon). Reports submitted are reviewed by community administrators within 24 hours, and reports flagging child safety violations are reviewed immediately. Users may also report content directly by email to the address listed under "Contact" below.

In addition, every user has access to in-app blocking, allowing them to immediately prevent any other user from contacting them, viewing their content, or interacting with them in any way.

5. Moderation and Enforcement

Costa Blanca Connect is actively moderated by community administrators. We:

  • Review every user-generated report within 24 hours, prioritizing child safety reports
  • Immediately remove violating content and permanently suspend offending accounts
  • Preserve evidence and account records for law enforcement cooperation
  • Maintain a permanent ban list to prevent re-registration by terminated users

6. Cooperation with Law Enforcement

Costa Blanca Connect cooperates fully with Panamanian and international law enforcement investigations involving the protection of children. Reports involving suspected CSAM are reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the equivalent Panamanian authority, as required by law.
